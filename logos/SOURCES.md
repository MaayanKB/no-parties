## Current logos used under fair use (not free)

For these lists no free current logo exists. The current logo is shown under a fair-use claim (Israeli
Copyright Act 2007, section 19): a free, non-commercial civic page that uses each logo only to identify
the list, next to a statement that the page is not affiliated with or endorsed by any party, and with a
link for rights holders to ask for removal. Pending a lawyer's review.
